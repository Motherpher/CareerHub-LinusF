## Evidence classes
- `verified-current-cv`: may be used in applications as written or conservatively paraphrased.
- `verified-cultural-cv`: valid for cultural/creative surfaces.
- `governed-secondary-evidence`: usable for discovery and positioning; strong outcome claims must resolve to underlying case evidence.
- `verification-needed`: excluded from strong claims until resolved.

## Explicit exclusions
The hub intentionally excludes date of birth, personal identity number, street address, phone number and personal email from version-controlled files.
